Algorithm TowersOfHanoi(n, Source, Auxiliary, Destination)

Step 1: If n = 1, then
            Print "Move container 1 from Source to Destination"
            Return

Step 2: Call TowersOfHanoi(n-1, Source, Destination, Auxiliary)

Step 3: Print "Move container n from Source to Destination"

Step 4: Call TowersOfHanoi(n-1, Auxiliary, Source, Destination)

Step 5: Stop
